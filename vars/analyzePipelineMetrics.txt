Analyze build history, stage timings, anomalies, and recommendations for a Jenkins Pipeline job.
Accepts jobName (default: env.JOB_NAME), historyLimit (default: 100), and includeRecommendations
(default: true). Can also be invoked with a single job name string: analyzePipelineMetrics('my-job').
Returns a plain serializable map with summary, anomalies, and recommendations.
