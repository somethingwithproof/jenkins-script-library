Create a Jenkins controller configuration backup or preview/apply a selective restore.
Parameters: destination (exact archive or directory path) or destinationDir (parent directory),
includeJobs (default: true), includePlugins (default: true), includeNodes (default: true),
compress (default: true), and archive (default: false). Call backupJenkinsConfig.restore(
backupFile:, files:, dryRun:, listContents:) to inspect or restore entries on the controller.
