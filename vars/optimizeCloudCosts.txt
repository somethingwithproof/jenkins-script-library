Analyze Jenkins cloud agent costs, check budgets, inspect allocations, or plan optimizations.
Parameters: action (analyze, optimize, allocate, or budget; default: analyze), dryRun (default: true
for optimize), and budgets (map of scope to budget amount for budget). Helper methods: .analyze(),
.optimize(dryRun:), .getAllocations(), .checkBudgets(Map), and .generateReport().
